# Beachside Church

**We help make faith practical.**

We're a church in Fort Walton Beach, Florida, for people across the Emerald Coast—locals, military families, and those just beginning to call this place home. We want to help you find real friends, a great place for your kids, and a faith that makes sense for everyday life.

Our mission is to inspire people to follow Jesus with all of their heart. We believe following Jesus makes life better and makes us better at life.

## A place to belong

Come as you are. Bring your questions. You can belong here before you believe.

From Waumba Land and UpStreet for kids to Transit and InsideOut for students, we create environments where the next generation can grow in faith. Community groups help adults build friendships, and Starting Point makes room for honest conversations about faith.

## FOR the Emerald Coast

We want to be known for what we are **FOR**. We love our community and partner with local nonprofits to serve our neighbors, support families, and put love into action.

[See how we're FOR the Emerald Coast](https://beachside.org/for/)
